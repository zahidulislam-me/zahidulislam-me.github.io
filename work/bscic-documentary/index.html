Research & Documentary / October 2025

BSCIC Tannery Estate

Field documentary and talk show

Watch the talk show & documentary

Objective

Explore the challenges, successes, and environmental and economic impact of the BSCIC Tannery Industrial Estate in Savar through a talk show and documentary for Project Management (309).

My Role

  • Fieldwork: Visited the project site with the team and spoke with industry professionals.
  • Research: Explored the estate’s environmental and economic impact and real-world project challenges.
  • Video Production: Collaborated with teammates across planning, filming, and editing.

Outcome

Our team completed a talk show and documentary in October 2025, based on site visits and conversations with industry professionals.

Learning

My first video on a real-world topic strengthened my teamwork skills and deepened my understanding of project management through firsthand observation.

Project Context

I am grateful to Md. Muzahidul Islam, Assistant Professor, Department of Marketing, for assigning this practical topic and for his guidance and encouragement. I also thank my teammates, Md. Aminul Islam and Md. Mozammel Haque Tanvir, for their commitment across planning, filming, and editing. The final result was a team effort.
