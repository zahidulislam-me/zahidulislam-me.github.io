ফ্রেম-এ বন্দি

Building an online sunglasses brand

Objective

Create and launch an online-only sunglasses brand for Strategic Brand Management (MKT-408), using STP, SWOT, and a brand mantra to guide business decisions.

My Role

  • Social Media: Created and set up the brand page before launch; maintained consistent posts.
  • Customer Communication: Handled DMs about fit, delivery, and product questions.
  • Sourcing & Pricing: Sourced quality products at low cost to support fair pricing and healthy margins.
  • Inventory: Kept online product availability aligned with actual stock.

Outcome

Our team launched ফ্রেম-এ বন্দি and presented the brand to faculty, receiving industry feedback on how branding decisions scale.

Learning

Brand identity works best when pricing, customer communication, margins, and product availability support the same promise.

Project Context

We presented to Professor Md. Kashedul Wahab Tuhin and Assistant Professor Sabakun Naher Shetu. Auvik Jamil, owner of J-The Story of Handloom Jamdani, provided feedback on scaling branding decisions.
