Brand Activation / 7 September 2025

Ornabites

A one-day marketing plan in action

Objective

Develop and execute a one-day marketing plan with Team Syncforce at the New Product Fair on 7 September 2025, as part of Marketing Management (MKT-401).

My Role

  • Brand Promotion: Promoted Ornabites through social media before the fair to generate customer interest.
  • Visual Identity: Designed the menu, banner, brand identity, and stall visuals.
  • Logistics & Sales: Coordinated supplies and logistics, and sold my self-developed product.
  • Session Hosting: Hosted the special session with faculty members and industry professionals.

Outcome

All food and ornament products sold out ahead of schedule during the one-day event.

Learning

The experience strengthened my planning, teamwork, customer communication, sales, and event coordination skills. It showed me how promotion and operational decisions influence customer response.

Project Context

Our stall offered food items and ornaments under the tagline “If you bite it, you write it.”
