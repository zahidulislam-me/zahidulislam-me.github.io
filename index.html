About Me

My Professional Focus

I’m interested in why people choose one brand over another—what they value, what earns their trust, and what brings them back. I want to use that understanding to help build brands that connect with consumers.

Building ফ্রেম-এ বন্দি made branding practical for me. Answering customer questions, choosing products, and setting prices showed me how everyday business decisions shape the way people experience a brand.

I enjoy turning ideas into action and learning from the response. As I begin my career, I want to bring that curiosity, teamwork, and willingness to learn into brand marketing.

Career Interests

My career direction is Brand Marketing and Brand Management, with an interest in developing my knowledge of Consumer Insights. These are areas I aim to grow into, supported by my practical experience in trade marketing and brand projects.
